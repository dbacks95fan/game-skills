# Official derived notes for rapid navigation

These are concise operational notes derived from the official materials in [rules-catalog.md](rules-catalog.md). They are navigation aids, not replacements for rulebook wording or card text.

## Turn and timing (Blood Money full game)

- The structure remains start of turn, action phase, and end of turn; expansion effects add checks rather than a new turn system.
- At start of turn, resolve start-of-turn effects, then choose the normal income/cards option and choose weapon/mount for the turn. The Blood Money rulebook also lists train movement within this phase.
- In the action phase, legal categories include move, card action, fight, location action, and—when Blood Money is active—trader action. The risk die may be used once per action phase, at any time **except during resolution of an action**.
- Resolve end-of-turn effects before end-position story conditions; then resolve a completed story, Wanted LP, and end-game trigger checks in the rulebook’s order.

## Blood Money replacements and additions

- Legendary Stories replace the base story-card system. Use the story overlay, Legendary Story deck, story track, and risk die as specified.
- A player who reaches the final story-track space stops there and chooses the listed track/LP reward; do not carry over excess story points.
- Risk die: announce it, choose one listed bonus, roll, resolve its story-point/wound result, then receive the chosen bonus. It is voluntary and once per action phase.
- Injury: after gaining wounds beyond the maximum, draw one injury. A player can have at most one injury. While already injured, each later wound requires discarding one poker card per wound. Losing all wounds removes/shuffles the injury as instructed; injury cards do not count as wounds.
- Traveling Trader: a trader action is available from any space in the cart’s indicated region. Legendary items cost $40 and the trader moves after the action. Verify card/stand availability for ordinary items.
- Ruined locations cannot be used for claim, location, or work actions. Repair occurs at the location (with the adjacency exception named in the rulebook), costs a poker-card discard after the action, removes the ruin, and grants story points.
- Claiming a deed costs its printed price at its matching location. A claimed deed is exclusive. Its action side is once per game; flip it after use. A ruined corresponding location blocks both using that ability and end-game scoring for that deed.

## End game (Blood Money)

- When the threshold is reached, complete the current round and then give every player one additional turn, including the triggering player.
- Final scoring adds upgrades, qualifying Legendary Items, cash conversion, wounds, injury losses, Wanted/Marshal scoring, and deeds as directed. For the exact score values and tiebreakers, read the current rulebook/compendium rather than rely on memory.

## Questions that require exact text

Open the relevant official rule or request the precise card/photo before ruling on: card timing, a character’s exception, variant setup, train encounter effects, Faro/poker outcomes, Sheriff/Marshal movement, specific story results, title cards, Bandit/Man in Black modules, or a component-edition difference.
