## Official-ruling versus community evidence

The publisher’s rulebooks and an answer directly attributable to Kolossal Games or its designers/developers are official. A fan compilation of such answers is still a *secondary* source: use it to find the original answer, identify it as a compilation, and do not elevate unsupported editor interpretation to a rule.

Useful secondary locator:

- [Rules of the West: A Western Legends FAQ – Official Answers](https://boardgamegeek.com/filepage/266042/rules-of-the-west-a-western-legends-faq-official-a) — community-maintained compilation that says its entries come from official sources and links originating discussions. Verify the linked primary answer when a precise ruling matters.

## Dependency and replacement checks

- `Base game` is required for all expansions.
- `Ante Up` is required by `Blood Money`.
- `Wild Bunch of Extras` relies on previous releases as its rules sheet specifies; confirm the individual module before setup.
- Do not assume all content is active merely because it is owned. Ask which variants/modules were set up.
- In a full Blood Money game, use its Legendary Stories and player aids rather than combining them with the earlier story system/aids.

## Retrieval discipline

Use a publisher link first. If it is unavailable, an identifiable publisher-uploaded BGG file or a reputable hosted scan may help the table read its own rulebook, but label it as a mirror and do not assume it is the newest printing. Do not download, store, redistribute, or quote substantial manual text in skill output.
